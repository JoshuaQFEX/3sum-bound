\begin{abstract}
We refine the numerical certificates and exponent margins in the published
ternary 3SUM formalization, establishing the exact rational exponent
$4800699/2500000=1.9202796$, below the listed bound $1.9203$.
The probability profile and algorithmic construction are inherited; the
contribution is a more precise proof of their running time. Exact rational
logarithm enclosures put every principal cost below $1.92027946$.
The finite-size and execution overheads fit within successive strict margins
ending at $1.9202796$. We provide the source patch, certificates, and
reproduction instructions. The construction and its original formalization
remain credited to their authors.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem and scope}
The benchmark asks whether an input list of $n$ integers contains three
distinct positions whose values sum to zero. Equal values at different
positions are permitted. For every fixed natural number $\kappa$, the input
magnitudes are at most $n^\kappa$. The conclusion supplies a single finite
deterministic word-RAM program for that $\kappa$, independent of $n$, the
input values, and the admissible word width. The minimum word width is
$b(\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor+1)$ for a fixed constant $b$.

The underlying statement, arithmetic instructions, indirect memory access,
branch semantics, and compiler are the published benchmark definitions.
This refinement concerns their asymptotic upper bound. It does not establish
a practical implementation, crossover size, or measured speedup.

The base source is the immutable revision \texttt{\basecommit} of
\texttt{fringewidth/three-sum-lean}, the source linked by the $1.9203$
leaderboard entry. The refined bound is
\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{3SUM}\in O\!\left(n^{4800699/2500000}\right)
       =O(n^{1.9202796}).}
\]
The exponent difference is exactly $0.0000204$. This is a tightening of the
analysis of the existing construction; the finite constants chosen by its
existence proofs may change when their admissibility conditions are tightened.

\section{The inherited parameter profile}
Use the exact decimal rationals
\[
 \pi=(0.81204903,\;0.15860350,\;0.02934747),\qquad
 \alpha=(0.90113327,\;0.08908424,\;0.00978249),
\]
\[
 \delta=0.01344845,\qquad y=0.18162395,\qquad z=0.07523953.
\]
They satisfy $\sum\pi_i=\sum\alpha_i=1$ and
\[
 \alpha_0=\pi_0+\pi_1/2+\pi_2/3,\quad
 \alpha_1=\pi_1/2+\pi_2/3,\quad \alpha_2=\pi_2/3.
\]
Writing $\mu=\pi_1+2\pi_2$, define the five quantities already used in the
source, with all logarithms in base two:
\begin{align*}
 H&=-\sum_{i=0}^{2}\pi_i\log_2\pi_i,&
 A&=-\sum_{i=0}^{2}\alpha_i\log_2\alpha_i,\\
 G&=\pi_1+\pi_2\log_2 3,\\
 B&=\log_2(1+y+y^2)-(\mu-\delta)\log_2 y,\\
 Q&=\pi_1\log_2(1+z)+\pi_2\log_2(1+z+z^2)
       -\delta\log_2 z.
\end{align*}
The principal cost ledger in \texttt{TernaryParameters.lean} is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ledger}
 \max\left\{\frac{2B}{H},\;
 2-\frac{G}{H}+\frac{2Q}{H},\;
 \frac{2A}{H},\;1+\frac{G}{H},\;2-\frac{G}{H},\;1\right\}.
\end{equation}
The first two entries are the active costs, for the stored bank and queries.
The other entries retain substantial room. The numerical ledger alone is
not a runtime theorem: the later executable cost proofs are also required.

\section{Exact logarithm and entropy certificates}
In this section $\ln$ denotes the natural logarithm. For positive rational
$x$, put $t=(x-1)/(x+1)$. The finite expansion
\[
 S_N(t)=2\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}\frac{t^{2j+1}}{2j+1}
\]
has the rigorous enclosure used by the existing Mathlib theorem
\[
 |\ln x-S_N(t)|\leq \frac{2|t|^{2N+1}}{1-t^2}.
\]
This deliberately uses the precise remainder provided by
\texttt{Real.sum\_range\_sub\_log\_div\_le}. All quantities on the right
are rational. Arguments are normalized by powers of two, and the resulting
logarithm bounds are combined with the enclosure of $\ln 2$ to obtain
the base-two entropy bounds.

We increase $N$ from 8 to 16 and round logarithm enclosures outward on a
$10^{-12}$ grid. The entropy intervals use a $10^{-11}$ grid. Rational
arithmetic, rather than floating-point evaluation, certifies the following
inclusive intervals:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule Quantity & Lower endpoint & Upper endpoint\\\midrule
$H$ & 0.81463563795 & 0.81463563796\\
$A$ & 0.51142981001 & 0.51142981003\\
$G$ & 0.20511813944 & 0.20511813945\\
$B$ & 0.78216403986 & 0.78216403988\\
$Q$ & 0.07008744368 & 0.07008744369\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
For the direct logarithm estimates, the largest coarse remainder is below
$4.048\times10^{-16}$. The independent check also verifies that each
declared rational interval contains the exact enclosure used by the Lean
proof, not only a high-precision numerical approximation to the logarithm.

Since $H>0$, cross-multiplication proves all ledger entries strictly below
$c=1.92027946$. For example, the two active inequalities reduce to
\[
 cH-2B>0,\qquad G-2Q-(2-c)H>0.
\]
Each is a linear rational inequality after inserting the appropriate interval
endpoints. The following upper bounds are rounded upward for display:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule Cost & Certified upper bound\\\midrule
$2B/H$ & 1.92027945610\\
$2-G/H+2Q/H$ & 1.92027938746\\
$2A/H$ & 1.25560382141\\
$1+G/H$ & 1.25179126704\\
$2-G/H$ & 1.74820873299\\
Constant term & 1\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
In particular, the exact bank bound is
$156432807976/81463563795$, which is strictly below $1.92027946$.
The unrounded exact fractions and positive cross-multiplication residuals
are included in the machine-readable certificate.

\section{Preserving the runtime overheads}
The refinement uses the following successive exponents:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule Stage & Exact decimal rational & Rational form\\\midrule
Principal certificate & 1.92027946 & $96013973/50000000$\\
Finite-block certificate & 1.92027957 & $192027957/100000000$\\
Frontend budget & 1.92027958 & $96013979/50000000$\\
Executed colored solver & 1.92027959 & $192027959/100000000$\\
Final benchmark & 1.92027960 & $4800699/2500000$\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The source already chooses a fixed $K\geq10^8$. Thus
\[
 \frac{10}{K}\leq10^{-7}<1.1\times10^{-7}
     =1.92027957-1.92027946.
\]
This covers both the $8/K$ and $10/K$ additions in the finite-block rate
proofs. Their eventual convergence arguments then select a sufficiently
large fixed block size.

The source's separate allowance is
\[
 \varepsilon_K=\frac1{100K^2}\leq10^{-18}<10^{-8}.
\]
Every subsequent gap in the table is $10^{-8}$. Hence the frontend and
executed-program inequalities retain strict room. The auxiliary runtime
parameter lemma also remains valid: its left side is a full exponent
strictly below the frontend budget, plus $\varepsilon_K+10^{-8}$, and
the final target is exactly $2\times10^{-8}$ above the frontend budget.

Finally, the existing \texttt{PowerBound} predicate permits arbitrarily
small positive slack. The already proved hybrid and compiler bridges
convert the actual executed-program bound at $1.92027959$ into the exact
rational benchmark at $1.9202796$. This step does not assert that a
$n^{a+o(1)}$ estimate implies an exact $O(n^a)$ estimate at its endpoint.

\section{Source changes and reproducibility}
The patch changes eleven files. It tightens logarithm and entropy intervals,
the stage exponents, and the explicitly stated differences between them;
it then updates the final theorem and its export. The immutable benchmark
statement, machine and compiler sources, dependency manifest, and
probability profile are preserved. Historical milestone constants that
are unrelated to the exported theorem are retained.

\begin{samepage}
The final export is
\begin{verbatim}
theorem Race.bound :
    EndStatement.ThreeSum.SolvedInTime
      ((4800699 : Rat) / 2500000) := by
  exact ThreeSum.solvedInTime_4800699_2500000
\end{verbatim}
\end{samepage}
Use Lean \texttt{v4.33.1} and Mathlib revision
\texttt{0df444a360eaa60ab8c11dca51a86af692955474}.
The package's instructions apply the patch to the pinned base revision,
check exact rational certificates, build the complete submitted source,
and perform the permitted-axiom audit. A successful local check and an
official leaderboard acceptance are separate events.

\section{What the refinement leaves open}
For the fixed profile above, the same entropy certificate gives
\[
 \frac{156432807972}{81463563796}
 \;\leq\;\frac{2B}{H}\;\leq\;
 \frac{156432807976}{81463563795}.
\]
Thus this particular bank-cost expression lies between
$1.92027945602$ and $1.92027945610$. The remaining gap to the exported
exponent is principally the retained runtime margins. More decimal
precision in the same logarithm estimates cannot remove those margins
or reduce this fixed cost expression substantially. Further progress may
come from adjusting the parameter profile, proving smaller overheads, or
changing the construction. These observations do not prove global
optimality of the profile or a lower bound for the 3SUM problem.
